Payout Policy

This Payout Policy explains how affiliate and partnership commissions are reviewed, approved, and paid within RECUR EMPIRE™.

1. Manual Payout Process

All payouts within the RECUR EMPIRE™ Affiliate Program are processed manually upon request. There are no automatic or guaranteed payouts.

2. Payout Requests

Affiliates must submit a payout request through the designated communication channel or partner interface. Requests may require identity verification, payment details, and compliance checks.

3. Review & Approval

Each payout request is subject to manual review. RECUR EMPIRE™ reserves the right to approve, delay, adjust, or deny payouts based on internal verification, fraud prevention, or compliance requirements.

4. Holding Period

Commissions may be subject to a holding or validation period to account for refunds, chargebacks, payment reversals, or subscription cancellations.

5. Minimum Payout Threshold

RECUR EMPIRE™ may establish minimum payout thresholds. Requests below the minimum threshold may be deferred until the threshold is reached.

6. Payment Methods

Approved payouts may be made via supported payment methods, including but not limited to digital wallets, bank transfers, or other third-party payment services. Available methods may change without notice.

7. Fees & Deductions

Transaction fees, processing fees, taxes, and third-party service charges may be deducted from payout amounts where applicable.

8. Reversals & Adjustments

RECUR EMPIRE™ reserves the right to reverse or adjust commissions resulting from refunds, fraudulent activity, abuse, or violations of Affiliate Terms.

9. No Liability for Delays

RECUR EMPIRE™ shall not be held liable for delays or failures in payouts caused by technical issues, third-party services, compliance reviews, or force majeure events.

10. Policy Updates

This Payout Policy may be modified at any time. Continued participation in the affiliate or partnership program constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
