Product & Infrastructure Vision

Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the strategic direction of RECUR EMPIRE™. Timelines represent intent and vision, not guaranteed delivery dates.

Q1 · 2026

Foundation & Public Entry

  • Launch of pre-registration for core products
  • Release of the first free infrastructure product
  • Activation of official social media channels
  • Launch of RECUR CLUB as a subscription-based product
Q2 · 2026

Flagship Product Launch

  • Release of a professional author-driven product
  • Development of a unique system with no direct market analogs
  • Expansion of paid infrastructure offerings
Q3 · 2026

Monetization & Partnership Scaling

  • Launch of the CPA / Affiliate Partner Program
  • Introduction of recurring partner revenue models
  • Expansion of tracking and payout infrastructure
Q4 · 2026

Strategic Integrations

  • Start of development of partner solutions with key AI-system providers
  • Infrastructure-level integrations with external platforms
  • Preparation for proprietary AI product development
Q1 · 2027

Proprietary AI Development

  • Initiation of development of a proprietary AI product
  • Joint development with selected strategic AI partners
  • Focus on automation, decision systems, and recurring logic
Q2 · 2027

AI × Crypto Infrastructure

  • Development of a first-of-its-kind integration of AI with crypto solutions
  • Unified system for subscription payments and partner payouts
  • Implementation of bonus logic, contribution-based rewards, and participation shares
  • Flexible percentage models tied to ecosystem growth and involvement
This roadmap is subject to change based on strategic priorities, market conditions, technological advancements, and regulatory factors.
